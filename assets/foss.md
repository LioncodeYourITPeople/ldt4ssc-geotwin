# Free and open-source component inventory

A flat list of the components the platform runs on, each with its
licence.

- **Purpose.** Make the dependency and licence picture readable without
  opening every `requirements.txt`.
- **State.** Published as `docs/FOSS.txt`. It is a bill of materials,
  not an audit. Recorded in the
  [1 September 2026 asset update](../updates/2026-09-01-foss-inventory.md).
- **Licence.** The inventory is documentation (CC BY 4.0). Each listed
  component keeps the licence named on its line (Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD,
  AGPL, and others).
- **Location.** <https://github.com/LioncodeYourITPeople/GeoTwin-ELBG/blob/main/docs/FOSS.txt>
